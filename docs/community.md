# Community Guidelines

Information regarding the PM & AI Community Lab, operational rules, and engagement channels.

---

## 1. Purpose of the Community

The PM & AI Community Lab is a knowledge-sharing space for Project Managers, PMOs, and Tech Leads exploring the practical and safe application of generative AI in software delivery.

Key activities include:
- Repository release notes, prompt engineering tips, and toolkit updates
- Practical case studies comparing ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Claude Code
- Continuous learning roadmaps and structured training materials
- Best practices for organizational AI adoption and prompt governance

[Access the PM & AI Community Lab](https://techaide.jp/community/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repository&utm_campaign=ai_toolkit&utm_content=community_page)

---

## 2. Community Code of Conduct & Scope

### In-Scope Topics
- Technical questions on using the toolkit's AI Contexts, Instructions, and Skills
- Prompt engineering methodologies and evaluation criteria
- General PM best practices for Agile/Scrum, PMO frameworks, and governance
- Feedback and improvement suggestions for the toolkit

### Strictly Out of Scope
- Direct consulting or operational advice on real, confidential client projects
- Sharing confidential business data, proprietary source code, or NDA-restricted information
- Personalized technical support for proprietary enterprise infrastructures

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Never post client names, corporate identities, personal data, or confidential details in community channels.
